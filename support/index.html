If you are in crisis or in danger right now, call your local emergency number (US and Canada 911, UK 999, Australia 000, New Zealand 111, Singapore 995, South Korea 119). In the app, Settings lists support services for your region.

Restore a purchase

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy. Purchases on iPhone and on Android are separate and can’t be moved between them.

Cancel a subscription

Cancelling is done in the store. In the app, Settings › Manage subscription opens the right page.

  • iPhone: Settings app › your name › Subscriptions › Spoon Down › Cancel Subscription.
  • Android: Google Play app › profile icon › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions › Spoon Down › Cancel subscription.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid the next charge. Plus stays on until the end of the period you paid for, and the free features stay as they are.

Refunds

Payments and refunds are handled by Apple and Google. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com (App Store) or through Google Play’s refund page. If you get stuck, email us.

14 days of Plus, free

New installs start with 14 days of Plus. We never ask for payment details, so nothing is charged when it ends; the app simply goes back to free and your records stay. Days left are shown in Settings.

Move your records to a new phone

  • Same kind of phone: your records come along in your phone’s own backup or transfer. Health-app values are read again on the new phone.
  • iPhone ↔ Android, or just to be safe: Settings › Data › Export to a file, then on the new phone, Settings › Data › Import a file.

Delete your records

Settings › Data › Erase all records deletes what you recorded in the app. It can’t be undone. Deleting the app also removes its data from your phone. Files you exported stay wherever you saved them.

Disconnect Apple Health or Health Connect

  • iPhone: in the Health app, tap your profile picture › Apps › Spoon Down, and turn off the categories.
  • Android: open Health Connect (from Settings, or the Health Connect app) › App permissions › Spoon Down.

The app only reads from the health app and never writes to it.

Reminders don’t arrive

Check that notifications for Spoon Down are on in your phone’s settings and that a Focus or Do Not Disturb mode isn’t silencing them. On Android, battery optimization can delay reminders.
