1. Who we are

Spoon Down is made by SoMa Inc. (representative: Changjae Lee), Iseong Bldg., 298 Dongho-ro, Jung-gu, Seoul 04617, Republic of Korea. Contact: support@soma.ai.kr. This policy covers the Spoon Down app for iPhone and Android and this website.

2. What stays on your phone

The app keeps the following in its own storage on your phone. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else.

WhatWhy
Your goal, weights, meals (time and size), Craving SOS entries (time, chosen food and mood, result), your note on why you’re dieting, and settingsTo show your body state, 7-day trend, reports and reminders
Body photos you take or choose (optional)To look back on your progress. Photos are never included in export files
A copy of values read from Apple Health or Health ConnectFor your trend and today’s checklist. Kept as a cache and left out of phone backups
Values shown in widgets and Live Activities (body state, time since your last meal, hold timer)To show widgets. Kept in storage shared only between the app and its own widgets
Plus status and the start date of the 14 days of PlusTo know whether Plus is on. On iPhone the start date is kept in the Keychain on this device only, so reinstalling doesn’t restart it

Data leaves your phone only when you choose: when you export a file, share a card, or through your phone’s own backup (section 6).

3. Apple Health and Health Connect

  • Read only, with your permission: weight, steps, workouts and sleep; on iPhone also waist circumference. On Android the app also asks to read history older than 30 days, so the first sync can bring in the last 90 days.
  • Used only inside the app to fill in your weight trend, today’s checklist and reports.
  • Never written to the health app, used for advertising or marketing, sold, sent to us or any third party, or stored in iCloud by the app.
  • Spoon Down’s use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • You can turn access off at any time. iPhone: Health app › profile picture › Apps › Spoon Down. Android: Health Connect › App permissions › Spoon Down.

4. Reminders, widgets and Live Activities

Reminders are scheduled on your phone; there is no push server. Widgets, the Lock Screen and Live Activities can show your body state, the time since your last meal and the hold timer to anyone who can see your screen. The quiet widget shows only a spoon and the time.

5. Purchases

Payments are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own privacy policies. We never receive your card or account details. The app only receives what it needs from the store to switch Plus on: which product is active and when it renews or ends. Offer codes are redeemed in the store’s own screen, and rating requests use the system’s own prompt.

6. Exports, sharing and backups

  • Export: you choose where to save the file (a backup file, or a weight CSV). Health-app values are included only if you tick that option. Photos are not included.
  • Share cards: made on your phone and shared only when you tap Share, through your phone’s share sheet. Current weight and dates are off by default.
  • Phone backups: your records can be included in your phone’s own backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup on iPhone; Google account backup or device-to-device transfer on Android), depending on your phone’s settings. The copy of health-app values is never included. The app never writes to iCloud Drive or Google Drive itself. Backups are handled by Apple or Google, and we cannot access them.

7. Deleting your data

Settings › Data › Erase all records deletes what you recorded in the app. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your phone. Files you exported and phone backups stay where they are until you delete them. Data in Apple Health or Health Connect stays there, because the app never writes to it; manage it in the health app.

8. If you contact us

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply. We delete them within one year after the conversation ends, unless the law requires us to keep them longer. We never use them for marketing.

9. This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log visitors’ IP addresses for security (GitHub Privacy Statement). Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when a page loads (Google Privacy Policy).

10. Children

Spoon Down is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

11. Security

Your records are protected by your phone’s app sandbox and storage encryption. Please use a screen lock. Exported files are not encrypted, so keep them somewhere safe.

12. Your rights where you live

Because your records stay on your phone, you can view, correct and delete them yourself at any time in the app. You can also email us about any privacy question.
