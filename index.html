When a craving hits, hold for 15 minutes.

A diet app that doesn’t count calories. Log a meal with one tap, follow your 7-day weight trend, and get through cravings between meals, 15 minutes at a time.

iPhone · iOS 18 or laterApp Store Android 14 or laterGoogle Play

Eat your meals as usual. Spoon Down helps you skip the snacking in between.

What it does

  • 01

    Body-state clock

    Time since your last meal shows where you are: Digesting · Diet mode · Fat-burn focus · Long fast. The times are general estimates, and everyone is different.

  • 02

    Craving SOS

    When a craving hits, start a 15-min hold with one small thing to do, such as a 3-minute mini-game (40 original one-button games). If you still want to eat afterwards, the app won’t stop you.

  • 03

    Rotten Delivery

    Order the food you’re craving for nothing. It has arrived. But… it’s rotten. What you would have spent adds up as Money saved. The rotten picture is optional.

  • 04

    Craving hour

    From your own records, the app finds the hour your cravings tend to hit and checks in about 30 minutes before: “Not now, right? Spoon down.”

  • 05

    Widgets and Lock Screen

    See your body state and the time since your last meal without opening the app, on Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets and in Live Activities on iPhone. A quiet widget shows only a spoon and the time.

  • 06

    Your records stay on your phone

    No account, no server, no ads, no analytics. With your permission, the app reads weight, steps, workouts and sleep from Apple Health or Health Connect. It never writes to them.

Free and Plus

Everything you use every day is free, and features that are free today stay free. Plus adds smarter, more personal tools such as adaptive reminders and detailed patterns.

14 days of Plus, free. New installs start with Plus switched on. No payment details, no automatic charge. When the 14 days end, the app simply goes back to free and your records stay.

Plus is available as a monthly or yearly subscription (auto-renewing) or as a one-time lifetime purchase. Prices are shown in the App Store and Google Play.
